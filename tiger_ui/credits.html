The research behind TIGER

ICLR 2025

Time-frequency Interleaved Gain Extraction and Reconstruction

TIGER is a lightweight audio separation model. It divides sound into frequency bands and combines information across time and frequency to reconstruct individual sources.

Here, TIGER-DnR separates dialogue, music and effects; the speech model separates two overlapping voices.

TIGER: Time-frequency Interleaved Gain Extraction and Reconstruction for Efficient Speech Separation
